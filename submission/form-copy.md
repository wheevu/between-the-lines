## Project name — 8 words

**Between the Lines: Multilingual News, Meaning, and Evidence**

## Project description — 135 words

People who read news across Vietnamese, English, and Korean can miss changes in
attribution, certainty, dates, and numbers because fluent translation can hide
ambiguity. Between the Lines translates one article into the reader’s chosen
language, marks exact wording worth checking with grounded language notes and
deterministic fact-preservation checks, and lets the reader choose one factual
claim to investigate. Claim Trace searches across all three languages, groups
repeated reporting, ranks evidence passages locally with multilingual E5, and
shows what independent sources support, dispute, or cannot establish. Human
reviewers keep control: no claim is preselected, exact excerpts and limitations
remain visible, and insufficient evidence produces abstention. Cloud models
handle contextual translation, query generation, and evidence comparison;
local processing handles bounded extraction, duplicate grouping, and evidence
ranking. Evaluation uses balanced tri-language news, bilingual feedback, and
independently adjudicated evidence.

## Evidence references for the problem statement

- [CONCRETE: Improving Cross-lingual Fact-checking with Cross-lingual
  Retrieval](https://aclanthology.org/2022.coling-1.86/) reports that
  fact-checking research has concentrated on English and that evidence in
  different languages can be complementary.
- [EACL 2026 language and retrieval bias
  study](https://aclanthology.org/2026.eacl-long.240/) reports persistent
  language bias and retrieval bias in multilingual fact-checked-claim
  detection.
- [SemEval-2025 multilingual and cross-lingual claim retrieval
  task](https://aclanthology.org/2025.semeval-1.323/) documents a substantial
  research benchmark devoted to retrieving matching fact-checked claims across
  languages.

These sources establish a cross-language retrieval problem. They do not prove
that Between the Lines solves it; that requires the project’s pending human and
factual-evidence evaluation.
