# Reviewer outreach and session brief

Do not ask one person to perform every role. Translation review, gold-evidence
adjudication, and reader observation answer different questions.

## Translation reviewer request

> I’m testing a student prototype that translates news among Vietnamese,
> English, and Korean and highlights phrases that may need a closer read. I need
> a bilingual reviewer—not a general product endorsement—to inspect a small
> frozen set. You’ll compare original and translated passages, label each
> surfaced note Yes, No, or Unsure, and write down important issues the system
> missed. It should take about 25–35 minutes. Your name is not stored; please
> choose a pseudonymous reviewer ID. The article text is ordinary public news,
> not personal or high-stakes material.

Minimum skill needed:

- Korean–English reviewer for KO↔EN;
- Vietnamese–English reviewer for VI↔EN;
- a reviewer comfortable with the remaining VI↔KO outputs, or two reviewers
  who can triangulate them without pretending fluency.

## Claim-candidate reviewer request

> I’m evaluating whether a model can pull useful, source-faithful claims from
> English, Vietnamese, and Korean news. You’ll see the exact article quote and a
> standalone claim. For each row, judge whether the standalone wording is
> faithful, whether the claim is central and externally checkable, and whether
> its fact/attribution label is correct. You are not being asked whether the
> claim is true. Blank and Unsure answers are retained rather than treated as
> success.

Use the 21-row general-news sheet first. Use what that session teaches about the
instructions before beginning the separate 67-row balanced-selection sheet; do
not alter the frozen candidates.

## Evidence adjudicator request

> I need an independent answer key for 30 frozen news claims. Please search in a
> normal browser without looking at the prototype’s results. For each claim,
> record whether adequate evidence supports, disputes, mixes, or cannot resolve
> it; preserve exact short excerpts, URLs, publication dates, and why the
> sources are independent. An “insufficient” result needs notes on what you
> searched. Two adjudicators must agree or preserve the disagreement.

Important boundary: adjudicators create the reference evidence before the
system run. Copying system results into the gold file invalidates the study.

## Reader-session request

> I’m observing whether the interface communicates uncertainty clearly. Please
> use one completed example while thinking aloud. I’ll ask you to explain what
> changed in translation, choose a claim, identify the strongest source, and
> explain why one result remains unresolved. I’m testing the interface, not
> you. I’ll record task outcomes and hesitation, not personal information.

For one session, invite a keyboard-only or assistive-technology user if
possible. Do not describe that single session as universal accessibility
validation.

## Session record

For every reviewer, record only:

- pseudonymous ID;
- author or independent role;
- language competence relevant to that task;
- date and task completed;
- consent to retain their judgments;
- unexpected confusion or missing instruction.

Do not collect demographic details that are not needed for interpreting the
evaluation.
